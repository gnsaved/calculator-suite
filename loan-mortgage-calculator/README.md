# Loan / Mortgage Calculator (EMI)

A finance tool to estimate loan repayment details including monthly payment (EMI) and overall cost.

## Features
- Monthly payment (EMI) calculation
- Total payment and total interest
- Repayment breakdown (if your version includes a schedule/table)
- Extra payment effects (if implemented)

## Built With
- HTML5
- CSS3
- Vanilla JavaScript

## How to Run
Open `index.html` in your browser.

## Disclaimer
Outputs are estimates. Always confirm with your lender’s official repayment schedule.
